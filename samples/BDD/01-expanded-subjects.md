Here are five topics in the SWEBOK Guide that are most closely associated with Behavior‑Driven Development (BDD):

- **Acceptance Criteria–Based Requirements Specification**  
  BDD is introduced as one of two variants (alongside ATDD) of acceptance criteria–based requirements specification, which shifts ambiguity out of natural‑language requirements and into precise, testable scenarios.

- **Acceptance Test–Driven Development (ATDD)**  
  A sibling practice to BDD, ATDD also begins by defining acceptance tests before any design or code, emphasizing early collaboration between domain experts, testers, and developers.

- **Test‑First Programming (Test‑Driven Development)**  
  At the construction level, writing tests before code (TDD) underpins both ATDD and BDD. This “test‑first” mindset drives both unit‑level and acceptance‑level test automation.

- **Scenario‑Based Testing**  
  A testing technique that derives test cases from high‑level scenarios—exactly the “Given/When/Then” stories BDD uses—ensuring behavior is validated end‑to‑end.

- **Behavioral Design Descriptions**  
  In the design KA, UML activity, sequence and state‑chart diagrams capture dynamic, behavioral views of a system—complementing BDD’s focus on clear behavioral specifications.
